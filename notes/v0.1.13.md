Settings and navigation: a new nested sidebar (Home, Inbox, Needs attention, Me, Agents, Explorer, Work, Code, People, Settings) with a search box; Settings split into Your settings and Instance settings. Entity pages open on an Overview tab, with plain tabs, Activity covering history and pulses, and Permissions in a dialog. Needs attention shows your own area by default, with plain-language filters and a Parked tab. Friendly permission errors throughout. Team pages show direct members and sub-teams. Federation syncs nested items and work status, is hardened for internet use, and you can share an item with a partner and clear finished invites. Repositories: push an existing repository into an empty one. Run tests uses a repository's declared test command. Self-hosting: install.sh --domain sets up automatic HTTPS with Caddy, with a step-by-step server guide (docs/DEPLOY_TO_A_SERVER.md). Agents get their own persistent home folder, and guidance can be set on AI profiles, agents and projects.
